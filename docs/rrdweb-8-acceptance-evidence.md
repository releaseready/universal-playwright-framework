# RRDWEB-8 login acceptance evidence

## Scope and evidence boundary

This map compares the active login acceptance criteria in [RRDWEB-8](https://noisegatemusicstudios.atlassian.net/browse/RRDWEB-8) and clarification comment [10541](https://noisegatemusicstudios.atlassian.net/browse/RRDWEB-8?focusedCommentId=10541) with repository tests. The browser checks use owned local fixtures and synthetic values. They do not contact customer staging, use customer credentials, or establish that a customer account can sign in. The ticket remains **In Progress** until the separately authorized customer-login acceptance is performed.

The original RRDSCRUM-22 requirements explicitly marked historical/deferred in the ticket are outside the current one-login acceptance. Password visibility, keyboard use, and form validation are supporting synthetic UI coverage, not proof of customer authentication.

## Reused login-control tests

`features/login-ui.feature` and `features/steps/login-ui.steps.ts` already cover password reveal/hide without submission, keyboard activation and visible focus, required-field validation without submission, exactly one Enter submission, and redaction of interaction logs/dialog/locator text. These are the requested reusable owned-synthetic tests; no duplicate scenarios were added.

## Production defects and unresolved acceptance

No production defect was established by this offline comparison. In particular, a blocked transport, synthetic 428 response, or degraded background feature is not evidence of bad credentials or a customer product defect. No production code was changed.

Remaining acceptance gates are the reviewed POST `/auth/login` and GET `/me` status/identity contract, customer-grounded marker and overlay locators, and specifically authorized single-attempt customer login evidence. Any onboarding/CAPTCHA/MFA path must remain not-verified/additional-interaction-required without an approved next step. Synthetic checks prepare the evidence path; they do not prove customer acceptance or justify moving RRDWEB-8 out of **In Progress**.
